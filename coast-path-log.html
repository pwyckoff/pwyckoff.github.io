      Getting your files

      Strava, whole history

      1. On strava.com in a browser (not the app), open Settings, then My Account.
      2. Under Download your account, choose Get Started and request your archive.
      3. Strava emails a download link, usually within a few hours. Save the .zip.
      4. Drop the .zip here as it is, without unzipping.

      Strava, one activity

      Open it on strava.com, choose the ⋯ menu, then Export GPX.

      Garmin

      In Garmin Connect on the web, open Account Settings and request Export Your Data. Garmin emails a .zip; drop it here as it is. Single activities can be exported from Garmin Connect too.

      Archive too big for the browser? Unzip it and drop the files from its activities folder together with activities.csv.
