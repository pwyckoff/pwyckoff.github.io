I teach on public policy courses at the LSE, including GV263, and on modules for Master's degrees in public policy such as PP4J1E and PP409E.

I also teach on Politics and Policy of Climate Change and Sustainability, a six-week LSE Executive Education online certificate course run by the Department of Government. It covers the political economy of climate action and inaction, the Just Transition, net-zero commitments and corporate climate governance, and is aimed at policymakers, advocates and practitioners.

Students can sign up for office hours on Student Hub.
