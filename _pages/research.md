My research studies the politics of climate policy and the low-carbon transition: how high-carbon jobs shape legislative voting, how publics respond to the employment costs of decarbonisation, how climate movements influence mainstream parties, and how electoral cycles affect the delivery of public transit. My PhD dissertation at the LSE was titled *Essays on Structural Change, with a Focus on Employment and Decarbonization* (2026).
